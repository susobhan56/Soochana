    About these numbers

    • Source. UNFPA, District Level Population Projections in Selected States of India – 2021 & 2026, published 2022. These are projections, not counts: they assume recent trends in births, deaths and migration carry on.
    • Odisha. The state figures on this page add up the 30 districts. They differ from ORGI's state projection by under 1,000 people because the two use different methods.
    • Eligible women is UNFPA's estimate of women aged 15–49 who form the base for family-planning services (the eligible couples). It is smaller than all women aged 15–49, which the table also shows.
    • Anganwadi age counts children under 6 (ages 0 to 5), who are served by anganwadi centres under ICDS. School age is 6 to 14, the ages covered by the Right to Education Act.
    • Following the children. A child aged 8 in 2026 was aged 3 in 2021. The difference between the two counts is mostly migration and, for the youngest, deaths.
    • The age structure on Soochana's other district pages for 2031 and 2036 grows these 2026 figures at ORGI's state growth rate. See the repository for the data files.
