The state's headline figures, its policy milestones since 1952, and twenty modules on land, governance, people, health, schools and infrastructure.

The forces behind the numbers

Eight frames on a changing state

Every figure on this page is the trace of something happening to real households: a birth deferred, a clinic reached, a train taken out of the district. These are the forces the data is measuring.

Scale

4.57 Cr projected population 2021, rising to 4.90 Cr by 2036 (ORGI Technical Group projections, 2020). India's eleventh most populous state, spread across thirty districts.

Births and deaths

A birth rate of 15.8 against a death rate of 7.9 per thousand. Natural increase remains positive and is easing gradually each decade.

Health systems

Biju Swasthya Kalyan Yojana reaches 96 Lakhs Families. Infant mortality has fallen to 28 per thousand live births.

Nutrition

Stunting among children under five came down to 26.8% in NFHS-6 (2023–24, published May 2026), from 31.0% in NFHS-5 (2019–21). Life expectancy now averages 70.4 years.

Education

Literacy stands at 72.9% statewide (Census 2011), and Khordha leads at 86.9%. Women with ten or more years of schooling rose to 43.9% in NFHS-6 (2023–24, published May 2026), from 33.0% in NFHS-5 (2019–21).

Work

The working-age share peaks within this decade. Skills and jobs created now will carry the state through the decades that follow.

Urbanisation

18.5% urban today. The Bhubaneswar–Cuttack corridor is where most of the urban growth is taking place.

Dependency

The over-sixty share rises from 11.5% in 2021 to 17.0% by 2036 (ORGI Technical Group projections, 2020). That gives the state time to plan pensions, health and care.
