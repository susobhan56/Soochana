Soochana Learn

Machine learning, explained with Odisha's data

Six visual essays on the ideas behind prediction models. Each one uses the portal's own figures for Odisha's 30 districts (literacy, child health, fertility, tribal share and more) and runs every model live in your browser.

By DIU Team · Sources: Census 2011, NFHS-4 and NFHS-5, UDISE+, Government of Odisha

The data behind the explainers

Every explainer draws on one table: the portal's district profiles, mortality and nutrition figures, fertility estimates, population projections and school statistics, joined into indicators for each of the districts. The matrix below cross-tabulates them. Each square shows how closely two indicators move together across the districts.

Choose a square to see the districts behind it.

Correlation across the 30 districts
−1 move opposite+1 move together

Moving together is not causing A strong correlation says two indicators rise and fall together across districts. It does not say one drives the other: both may follow a third factor such as income, remoteness or when the district was surveyed. With only 30 districts, a correlation near ±0.3 can easily arise by chance.

About Soochana Learn

Inspired by MLU-Explain, Amazon's visual machine-learning essays, and rebuilt around Odisha's own statistics so that every example is a real district. The models are deliberately small and transparent: they are there to teach how prediction works and where it goes wrong, not to rank districts or guide funding. The modelling table is built by learn/tools/build_learn_data.py from files already in the portal. Download the table (JSON).
