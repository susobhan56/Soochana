01 — The Scale

The rise to the world's largest population

India's population grew from 376 million in 1950 to 1.428 billion in 2023. Unlike China, whose population has begun to decline, India will continue growing until peaking around 2060.

1.428B
Peak scale reached · 2023
02 — The Transition

Below replacement for the first time

A country needs a total fertility rate of 2.1 to maintain a stable population. India's fertility rate fell to 1.98 in 2023, signaling that population stabilization is on the horizon.

1.98
Fertility rate (TFR) · replacement = 2.1
