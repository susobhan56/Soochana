Add it to Gmail

  1. Put signature.gif in the root of your Soochana GitHub repo and push. It will be live at https://susobhan56.github.io/Soochana/signature.gif within a minute or two.
  2. Open this page, fill in your email and LinkedIn, and press Copy signature.
  3. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) › See all settings › General › Signature › Create new.
  4. Click in the signature box, paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V), then scroll down and press Save changes.
  5. Send yourself a test email to see the animation play.
