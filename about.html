Platform Overview

About Soochana

A digital ecosystem built to make demographic evidence accessible, contextual, and actionable.

Soochana is a digital knowledge platform that transforms demographic statistics into accessible narratives through interactive data visualizations, analytical stories, and evidence-based resources.

The platform enables policymakers, researchers, students, media professionals, and citizens to explore Odisha's demographic transition through dynamic dashboards, thematic articles, reports, datasets, and factsheets.

Rather than presenting numbers in isolation, Soochana connects data with context, making complex demographic changes easier to understand.

Dynamic Dashboards

Explore district-level population shifts, age distributions, and gender indicators interactively.

Access downloadable reports, factsheets, NFHS data, and Census publications.

Core Objectives

Why We Built Soochana

Population is constantly changing, and every demographic shift influences education, healthcare, employment, urbanization, ageing, and economic development.

01

Understand Demographic Trends

Gain comprehensive insights into fertility transition, mortality decline, age structural shifts, and urbanization patterns across Odisha.

02

Explore Interactive Visualizations

Engage with intuitive choropleth maps, comparative district trend lines, and population pyramids built for instant clarity.

03

Access Reliable Datasets & Reports

Download verified survey datasets from Census of India, NFHS-5, PLFS, UDISE+, and state survey repositories seamlessly.

04

Discover Evidence-Based Narratives

Read contextualized thematic articles examining how population dynamics intersect with public welfare, health, and economic growth.

05

Support Research & Policy Decisions

Provide researchers, policymakers, media, and development practitioners with empirical evidence for targeted interventions.

Institutional Foundations

Pioneering Demographic Intelligence

Soochana is powered by a strategic collaboration committed to evidence-based development and population research.

Analytical Lead

Demographic Intelligence Unit (DIU)

The Demographic Intelligence Unit (DIU) is an initiative of the Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD), established with technical support from UNFPA Odisha.

DIU works at the intersection of demographic research, data visualization, policy communication, and evidence generation to promote informed development planning in Odisha.

Its objective is to make demographic evidence more accessible for governments, researchers, development practitioners, media, and the public.

Founding Organisation

Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD)

The Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD) is a leading development organization based in Odisha, working since 1982 to promote inclusive, equitable, and sustainable development.

CYSD works across governance, livelihoods, disaster resilience, climate change, social inclusion, and policy advocacy while strengthening evidence-based development through research and innovation.

Technical Partner

United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA)

UNFPA is the United Nations agency dedicated to ensuring that every pregnancy is wanted, every childbirth is safe, and every young person's potential is fulfilled.

In Odisha, UNFPA supports evidence-based planning and policy by strengthening demographic intelligence, population research, gender equality, reproductive health, and youth development.
