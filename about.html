Soochana /suːˈtʃə.naː/ · noun · Odia — information; the act of making known

We turn a state's numbers into stories you can read, question, and act on.

Every figure in Odisha's demographic record is a life counted — a birth, a school, a household growing older. Soochana exists to give those figures their meaning back.

What we do

Our vision is an Odisha where every plan starts from an honest picture of its people.

Odisha's population is changing faster than most plans can keep up with. Families are smaller, people live longer, and more of them move for work. Soochana exists so that change is seen early and understood clearly.

We gather the state's demographic record from official sources, explain what it shows in plain language, and put it in the hands of the people who plan schools, hospitals, jobs and services.

We work with government, civil society, academia and the media, so that population is part of every development conversation, not an afterthought.

Questions we're often asked

How does Soochana turn data into understanding?

We start with official statistics and ask what they mean for people's lives. Every figure carries its source, its year, and whether it was counted or projected. Maps, charts and short stories then explain the change behind it, district by district.

Where does the data come from?

From published official sources: the Census of India, the National Family Health Survey, the Sample Registration System, the Periodic Labour Force Survey, UDISE+ school data, projections by the Office of the Registrar General of India, and Government of Odisha reports. The datasets and reports are in the Repository.

Is Soochana about controlling population?

No. Soochana describes how Odisha's population is changing; it does not argue for fewer people, or for more. We support voluntary, rights-based choices and reject coercion in any form. The questions we care about are about wellbeing: whether young people find work, whether older people are cared for, and whether women can make informed choices about their own lives.

Who is Soochana for?

Anyone who needs to understand Odisha's people: officials planning services, researchers and students, journalists checking a claim, and citizens curious about their own district. No statistics training is needed; every chart is explained in plain words.

Why is population so easy to misread?

A single number rarely tells the whole story. The population can keep growing after families become smaller, because so many young people are already born. A state average can hide very different districts, and a projection is an estimate, not a count. Soochana puts figures side by side and in context, so they are harder to misread and easier to use.

Numbers only matter when someone uses them.

Institutional Foundations

Pioneering Demographic Intelligence

Soochana is powered by a strategic collaboration committed to evidence-based development and population research.

Analytical Lead

Demographic Intelligence Unit (DIU)

The Demographic Intelligence Unit (DIU) is an initiative of the Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD), established with technical support from UNFPA Odisha.

DIU works at the intersection of demographic research, data visualization, policy communication, and evidence generation to promote informed development planning in Odisha.

Founding Organisation

Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD)

The Centre for Youth and Social Development (CYSD) is a non-governmental, non-profit organization established in 1982, has been working towards improving the quality of life of the tribal and rural poor by focusing on eradicating extreme poverty and hunger, and educating socio-economically distressed people to access their entitlements.

Technical Partner

United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA)

UNFPA, the United Nations Population Fund, is the sexual and reproductive health agency of the UN. It works to uphold the rights and choices of women, girls and young people across more than 150 countries. UNFPA aims to ensure that every pregnancy is wanted, every childbirth is safe, and every young person can fulfil their potential.
