Metric Analysis
I. Expansion

A Growing
Footprint

Over the last few decades, this district has experienced substantial population shifts. Toggling projected trajectories outlines the structural expansion expected through 2036.

--
Projected Population (2036)
II. Fertility

Smaller, Healthier
Families

The newest survey, NFHS-6 (2023–24, published May 2026), shows how family life is changing in the district, next to NFHS-5 (2019–21). Neither survey publishes a district fertility rate; for Odisha as a whole it is 1.7 children per woman in NFHS-6, from 1.8.

--
Children under 15, share of people (NFHS-6)
III. Longevity

Life Expectancy
Gains

Better nutrition, sanitation and health cover (such as BSKY) have lengthened lives, and women have gained the most between survey rounds. These figures are computed from NFHS-4 and NFHS-5; the NFHS-6 fact sheets (published May 2026) do not carry life expectancy.

Female life expectancy (computed from NFHS-5, 2019–21)
IV. Health

Decline in
Mortality Rates

Maternal and child health programmes have brought child deaths down. The chart compares NFHS-4 (2015–16) with NFHS-5 (2019–21); the NFHS-6 district fact sheets (published May 2026) do not report mortality rates.
